# Privacy Policy of the Privacy Test App

Last updated: January 2026

This privacy policy explains which personal data the Privacy Test App ("the app", "we")
collects, why we collect it and how long we keep it.

## 1. Location data

To show nearby points of interest, the app uses your location. We process your
latitude and longitude while the map screen is open. When you use the hiking mode,
we additionally record the altitude of your current position to calculate the
elevation profile of your route.

Location data is processed on your device and is only sent to our servers if you
explicitly share a route.

## 2. Contact and account data

When you enable call-back support, we read your phone number so that our support
team can call you back. We also read the list of registered device accounts to
offer a one-tap sign-in with an existing account.

## 3. Web content

The app embeds web pages. For these pages we store cookies to keep you signed in
and we transmit your browser user agent so that pages are displayed correctly.

## 4. Network information

To detect whether you are on a metered connection, we read information about your
wifi connections, including the IP address assigned to your device.

## 5. Storage and deletion

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above.
You can request deletion at any time by writing to privacy@example.org.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data,
and to object to or restrict its processing.
